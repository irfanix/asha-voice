# Child under 5 — quick reference for ASHA Didi

Source: IMNCI chart booklet (Integrated Management of Newborn and Childhood Illness), MoHFW. Verify against the current IMNCI chart before printing or distribution.

## General danger signs (refer the child TODAY if any are present)

- Unable to drink or breastfeed
- Vomits everything
- Convulsions / fits
- Lethargic or unconscious

If ANY one of these is present, the child has a general danger sign. Refer immediately to the PHC or the nearest hospital. Do not wait to see if the child gets better.

## Fast breathing thresholds (count for one full minute)

- Less than 2 months old: 60 breaths per minute or more
- 2 to 12 months old: 50 breaths per minute or more
- 12 months to 5 years old: 40 breaths per minute or more

Fast breathing with or without chest indrawing is a sign of possible pneumonia. Refer the child the same day.

## Severe dehydration (refer for ORS and IV fluids)

Look for:

- Sunken eyes
- No tears when crying
- Dry mouth and tongue
- Very slow skin pinch (goes back slowly)
- Lethargic or unconscious

If two or more of these are present with diarrhoea, refer the child today. Start ORS on the way if the child can drink.

## Diarrhoea with no dehydration (manage at home)

- Give extra fluids: ORS, rice water, buttermilk, clean water.
- Give zinc as per the current guidelines.
- Continue breastfeeding and normal food.
- Teach the family the signs of dehydration and when to come back.

## Routine immunisation schedule (Universal Immunisation Programme)

- At birth: BCG, OPV-0, Hepatitis B
- 6 weeks: OPV-1, Pentavalent-1, Rotavirus-1, PCV-1
- 10 weeks: OPV-2, Pentavalent-2, Rotavirus-2
- 14 weeks: OPV-3, Pentavalent-3, Rotavirus-3, PCV-2
- 9 to 12 months: Measles-Rubella-1, PCV booster, Vitamin A (first dose)
- 16 to 24 months: DPT booster-1, Measles-Rubella-2, Vitamin A (second dose)
- 5 to 6 years: DPT booster-2

Vitamin A is repeated every 6 months up to 5 years of age. Deworming (albendazole) is given every 6 months from 1 to 5 years.

## What to tell the family

- Feed the child small, frequent meals even during illness.
- Give ORS every time the child has loose stools.
- Do not stop breastfeeding during diarrhoea or fever.
- Bring the child to the PHC immediately if any danger sign appears.
- Keep the immunisation card safe and bring it to every visit.

## What ASHA Voice will not do

- It will not diagnose pneumonia, malaria or any other illness.
- It will not prescribe or dose any medicine.
- It will not decide whether a child needs antibiotics.

If in doubt, refer to the PHC.
