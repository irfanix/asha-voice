# Antenatal Care (ANC) — quick reference for ASHA Didi

Source: MoHFW Mother and Child Protection (MCP) card; National Health Mission (NHM) ASHA guidelines. Verify against the current MCP card before printing or distribution.

## Danger signs in pregnancy (refer the woman TODAY)

- Vaginal bleeding at any time in pregnancy
- Convulsions / fits
- Severe headache with blurred vision
- High fever
- Severe abdominal pain
- Swelling of the face or hands
- Difficulty breathing
- Reduced or absent baby movements
- Leaking of water before labour
- Blood pressure of 140/90 or above

If any of these are present, the woman must be taken to the PHC or the nearest hospital the same day. Call 108 if she cannot be moved safely by the family.

## Routine ANC care (for every pregnant woman)

- At least 4 ANC visits: around weeks 12, 20, 26 and 30, then fortnightly from week 36 up to delivery (MCP card schedule).
- Blood pressure, weight and haemoglobin checked at every visit.
- Iron and folic acid (IFA) tablets daily from the second trimester, for at least 180 days, and 180 days after delivery.
- Two doses of Td (or TT) as per the current schedule.
- One dose of albendazole after the first trimester, as per state guidelines.
- Calcium supplementation where recommended by the state.
- Counselling on danger signs, birth preparedness, institutional delivery and family planning.

## What to tell the family after a routine visit

- Eat one extra meal a day; rest when possible.
- Take IFA and calcium regularly with water, not with tea.
- Come to the PHC immediately if any danger sign appears.
- Plan where the delivery will happen and how to reach the facility.

## What ASHA Voice will not do

- It will not prescribe any medicine.
- It will not give a dose of any drug.
- It will not decide whether the pregnancy is "normal" — that is the doctor's or ANM's job.

If in doubt, refer to the PHC.
