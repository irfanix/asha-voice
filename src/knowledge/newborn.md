# Newborn care (0–42 days) — quick reference for ASHA Didi

Source: Home Based Newborn Care (HBNC) guidelines, MoHFW / NHM. Verify against the current HBNC module before printing or distribution.

## Danger signs in a newborn (refer the baby TODAY)

- Not feeding well, or stopped feeding altogether
- Convulsions / fits
- Fast breathing (60 breaths a minute or more)
- Severe chest indrawing (the lower chest pulls in with each breath)
- Temperature 37.5 °C or above, or below 35.5 °C
- Lethargic or unconscious
- Yellow palms or soles (severe jaundice)
- Redness or pus around the umbilical cord
- Very low birth weight (below 2.5 kg)

If any of these are present, the baby must be taken to the PHC or the nearest hospital the same day. Call 108 if the baby cannot be moved safely by the family.

## Routine newborn care (counselling the family)

- Keep the baby warm: skin-to-skin contact, cap, wrap, no cold draughts.
- Start breastfeeding within 1 hour of birth and breastfeed exclusively for the first 6 months.
- Feed on demand, day and night, at least 8 to 12 times in 24 hours.
- Keep the umbilical cord clean and dry. Apply nothing except chlorhexidine as per guidelines.
- Bath only after 24 hours. Sponge the baby with warm water.
- Do not apply kajal, oil or ash to the cord, eyes or skin.
- Weigh the baby at each HBNC visit.
- Ensure the baby receives BCG, OPV-0 and Hepatitis B at birth.
- Register the birth within 21 days.

## Home Based Newborn Care (HBNC) visit days

- Day 1 (only for home deliveries): first HBNC visit
- Day 3: HBNC visit
- Day 7: HBNC visit
- Day 14: HBNC visit
- Day 21: HBNC visit
- Day 28: HBNC visit
- Day 42: HBNC visit and mother's postnatal check

If a danger sign is found at any visit, do not wait for the next scheduled day — refer the baby immediately and record the referral in ASHA Voice.

## What to tell the family

- Breastfeed often; the baby needs to feed at least 8 times a day.
- Keep the baby warm, but do not over-wrap in hot weather.
- Wash hands before touching the baby, especially before feeding and after changing.
- Come back to the PHC immediately if any danger sign appears.

## What ASHA Voice will not do

- It will not diagnose any illness.
- It will not prescribe any medicine.
- It will not decide the dose of any vaccine or syrup.

If in doubt, refer to the PHC.
